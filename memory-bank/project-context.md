# Project Context - bbacvet.com

## Overview

**bbacvet.com** is a veterinary clinic website for Brighton Beach Animal Care, located in Brooklyn, NY. The website serves as a comprehensive online presence for the veterinary clinic, providing information about services, booking capabilities, and contact details.

## Business Purpose

- **Primary Function**: Veterinary clinic website showcasing services and enabling appointment booking
- **Target Audience**: Pet owners in the Brighton Beach/Brooklyn area
- **Key Services**: Veterinary care, wellness plans, vaccines, surgery, dentistry
- **Business Model**: Professional veterinary services with online appointment scheduling
